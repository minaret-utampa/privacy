The Minaret App Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how The Minaret app (“the app”) for iPhone and Android handles information. The app is made for The Minaret, the student newspaper of the University of Tampa (“we”, “us”). It covers the app only. The Minaret website has its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.
  • The only information that leaves your phone is what’s needed to show you stories and, if you turn them on, to send you notifications.

Information we don’t collect

The app does not collect or ask for:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other contact details
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • information about your activity in other apps or websites

The app doesn’t contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Information stored only on your phone

To work, the app stores these on your device:

  • stories you save, so you can read them offline
  • your settings, such as appearance and text size
  • the sections you chose for notifications
  • recently viewed images, so they load faster

This information stays on your phone and isn’t sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Loading stories

When you open the app, browse a section or search, the app requests stories from a server we run on Cloudflare, which gets them from The Minaret’s website (hosted by WordPress.com). These requests don’t include your name or any account, and they only ask for stories that are already public on our website.

Like any website, our server receives your device’s IP address, along with what you asked for, including words you search for. Cloudflare keeps these technical logs for a short time to keep the service running and fix problems. We don’t use them to identify or track readers.

Photos in stories load from WordPress.com’s image service, which also receives your device’s IP address when delivering them.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and Apple’s push notification service on iPhone) to deliver them. For this to work, the app shares with Firebase:

  • an anonymous device token, a random code that lets notifications reach your phone
  • the list of sections you chose to follow

Firebase also creates its own random installation ID for the app. None of this is connected to your name, email or any account. We use it only to send you notifications about new stories in the sections you chose.

You can change your sections in Settings → Notifications in the app, or turn off notifications entirely in your phone’s settings at any time.

Links and videos

Stories may link to other websites or to videos on services like YouTube. These open outside the app, in your browser or the service’s app, and those sites’ own privacy policies apply. The app doesn’t play videos inside itself.

Sharing your information

We don’t sell, rent or share your information. The only outside services involved are those described above, which we use to run the app:

  • Cloudflare (our server): cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Firebase (notifications): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • WordPress.com, by Automattic (our website and images): automattic.com/privacy
  • Apple and Google (delivering notifications to iPhone and Android)

We may disclose information if required by law.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience, including University of Tampa students, faculty and staff. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Security

The app’s server, notification service and code are kept on accounts managed for The Minaret, with access limited to the people who maintain the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
